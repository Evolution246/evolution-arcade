ONLINE SCORE DUEL · 2–4 PLAYERS

Everyone plays their own table at the same time with live scores. Highest score after 3 balls wins.

Controls: on a phone, hold the left and right buttons (or touch either side of the screen). On a computer, use Z and M. To launch, hold a flipper button to charge the plunger, then release. (Space works too.)
